Use Cases

Forge OS is best for:

  • Developers building AI-powered automation
  • Power users who want full control and privacy
  • Researchers experimenting with agent architectures
  • Teams building internal tools and workflows
  • Anyone who wants to run AI entirely on-device

ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini apps are better for:

  • Casual conversations and quick questions
  • Users who want a simple, polished interface
  • People who don't need local execution
  • Mobile-first users who prefer cloud convenience

Why Choose Forge OS?

  • Complete Control: Everything runs on your device. No cloud dependency.
  • Privacy First: Your data never leaves your phone. Keys are encrypted on-device.
  • Powerful Automation: Python runtime, file management, scheduling, and more.
  • Extensible: Plugins, MCP support, and sub-agents for complex workflows.
  • Open Source: Review the code, contribute, or build your own version.
  • Bring Your Own Provider: Use any LLM provider with automatic fallback chains.
  • Designed for Reliability: Works on flaky networks, dead keys, and while the screen is off.
